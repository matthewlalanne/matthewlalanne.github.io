About

Started in nonprofits. Still designing for the person on the other side of the screen.

I'm a product designer at Spectrum. I started in the nonprofit world, at a foundation that serves people with Down syndrome, and watching how much the right tool could change someone's day is a big part of why I moved into design.

At Spectrum I spent my first two years on internal tools, sitting with the agents and sales reps who used them and untangling the workflows that slowed them down. In 2024 I moved to the corporate website team and built out its components. Since 2025 I've been co-leading the site's redesign and working directly with executive stakeholders on where it goes next.

A lot of my recent work is in design operations. I build AI workflows that take on the large-scale audits and research synthesis our studio used to do by hand. The part I spend the most time on is making sure the output is right: I spot-check results against the source files, test the workflow on a sample where I already know the answer, and keep a human review step before anything reaches the team. AI makes the first pass cheap. Trusting it is still my job.

Outside work I'm usually building something. My current side project is Reality Check, a fantasy league app for reality TV that I designed and built with AI tools, and I'm testing it with friends before it launches.
