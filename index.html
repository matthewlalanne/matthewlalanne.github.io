Featured · Last Chair Studio · In beta

I tracked four Survivor leagues in a Figma file. Then I created the app that replaced it.

Reality Check runs a fantasy league around a reality show. You draft a cast, call who goes home each week, and talk about it in the league chat.

Four leagues of my friends and family have played it all season, and I’ve treated every episode as a test. I ship a change, watch how people use it that week, and keep what works. That’s how I found out why people kept missing their weekly pick, and how I fixed it.
