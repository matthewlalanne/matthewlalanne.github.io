2 of 4Competitive EdgeProduct designer2022 to 2023Password protected

Agents had a tool for comparing offers. They were using a calculator instead.

Agents compare offers while a customer waits on the line, and they’d stopped trusting the tool that was supposed to help. When I started, 6% of eligible agents were using it. In a week-long design sprint the team picked my layout, and it became the basis for the redesign. Thousands of agents use the tool.

3 of 4Multi-Tenant SalesLead designer2024 to 2025Password protected

A vendor ran our referral platform. I led the design of the one that replaced it.

The company wanted fewer vendors and more of its software in-house, starting with the referral platform leasing agents use. I led the design, with gift card rewards built in. Duplicate accounts came up in a user interview, not in the requirements, so I designed a check that flags them for a supervisor. We designed and built it in under a year.

4 of 4Spectrum corporate websiteCo-led the redesign2024 to nowIn progress

I designed four pages. Their components rebuilt the rest of the site.

I co-led the redesign of Home, History, About and the leadership bios, did most of the hands-on design, and designed the component system they’re built on. The rest of the landing pages were then rebuilt from those components, in order of page traffic. Nobody asked for authoring guides, but I’m building one for every component so authors know the character counts, image crops and how each one fits every screen size. Our content author and multimedia director already work from them.
